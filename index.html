Auscultation Trainer

HeartLung
Tap a site to listen

Practice tool for learning heart and lung sounds. Tap a spot on the chest to hear a recording from that site, then name what you hear. Quiz mode hides the answer until you tap Reveal, and the case menus let you drill one sound at a time.

Recordings come from HLS-CMDS (a clinical manikin), CirCor DigiScope (heart sounds from children, matched to the nearest site here) and the ICBHI 2017 database (lung sounds from patients). ICBHI only records left or right on the front of the chest, so its upper, mid and lower zones are arbitrary. Everything is from the front of the chest. For training only.
